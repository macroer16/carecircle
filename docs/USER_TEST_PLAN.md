# USER_TEST_PLAN.md

## Phase 1 — ~10 interviews (before or alongside beta)
Behavior-focused questions, not opinion-focused:
- "Walk me through the last time you and a sibling both thought the other had
  handled something for your parent — what happened?"
- "Show me where your parent's medication list actually lives right now."
- "How did you find out about the last change to your parent's care plan?"
- "If your sibling had to take over tomorrow, could they, from what currently
  exists in writing?"

**Behavioral signal that matters most:** ask them to physically show you their
current system. If they can't produce an organized answer in under 30 seconds,
that's the real pain point, observed rather than claimed.

## Phase 2 — 3-5 real family betas
Give real access, real onboarding, real use for at least 1-2 weeks — not a demo.

## The core comparative task
> "You haven't checked on [parent]'s care for seven days. Tell me what changed
> and what happens next."

Run this twice per family: once using their **current method** (WhatsApp/notes/
memory), once using **CareCircle**. Measure:
- Time to answer
- Number of follow-up questions/confusion
- Stated confidence in their answer
- Which method they say they'd actually choose to keep using

## Validation threshold (explicit, not arbitrary)
- 🟢 **Real signal to keep building:** CareCircle is meaningfully faster AND the
  family says they'd keep using it over WhatsApp after the test period ends
- 🟡 **Modify:** Technically faster but families still reach for WhatsApp out of
  habit — signals the switching-cost problem needs direct product attention
  (e.g. better notifications, easier daily habit formation)
- 🔴 **Stop and rethink:** No meaningful time/confidence difference, or families
  actively avoid using it during the trial period

## What to do with results
Update FINAL_STATUS.md and YC_ASSESSMENT.md with actual findings — replacing the
"zero real users" gap with real evidence, whichever direction it points.
